📋 How to Collect a Valid Soil Sample for Laboratory Testing:

  1. Walk in a zigzag pattern across your field plot and collect sub-samples from 8–10 spots.
  2. Scrape off surface organic debris. Make a V-shaped cut to a depth of 15 cm (plough depth).
  3. Collect a 1-inch slice of soil from the face of the V-cut.
  4. Mix all sub-samples thoroughly in a clean plastic bucket. Use quartering method to reduce to 500 grams.
  5. Air dry in shade (never under direct sun or heat), pack in a clean cloth bag, label with survey number, and submit to your nearest KVK or Government Soil Testing Laboratory.

🍃 Organic Farming & Indigenous Bio-Inputs Manual
Zero-Budget Natural Farming

Standardized, field-tested recipes for organic soil inoculants, biological pest repellents, and composting techniques.

🧪 Jeevamrutha Liquid Bio-Inoculant
Multiplies billions of beneficial soil microorganisms and activates fungal mycorrhiza.
  • Ingredients: 10kg Desi cow dung, 5-10L cow urine, 2kg organic jaggery, 2kg pulse flour (besan), handful of virgin forest soil, 200L water.
  • Process: Mix in shade, stir clockwise twice daily for 48–72 hours.
  • Application: Apply 200 Litres/acre with irrigation water every 15–20 days.
🌱 Beejamrutha (Organic Seed Treatment)
Protects young seedling radicles and plumules from seed-borne & soil-borne pathogens.
  • Ingredients: 5kg Cow dung, 5L Cow urine, 50g Lime (Chuna), handful virgin soil, 20L water.
  • Process: Soak overnight, strain liquid through cloth.
  • Application: Coat seeds evenly, air dry in shade before sowing.
🥛 Panchagavya Growth Promoter
Rich in auxins, gibberellins, and beneficial lactic acid bacteria.
  • Ingredients: Cow dung (7kg), Cow ghee (1kg), Cow urine (10L), Cow milk (3L), Cow curd (2L), Tender coconut water (3L), Sugarcane juice (3L), 12 ripe bananas.
  • Fermentation: Ferment for 21 days with daily morning and evening stirring.
  • Foliar Spray: 3% solution (300ml in 10L water).
🌿 NSKE 5% (Botanical Pest Repellent)
Natural antifeedant, insect growth regulator (IGR), and oviposition deterrent.
  • Ingredients: 5kg Good quality powdered neem seed kernels, 100L water, 100g soap nut or mild surfactant.
  • Process: Soak neem powder tied in cloth bag overnight, squeeze extract thoroughly.
  • Targets: Effective against whiteflies, aphids, jassids, and early instar caterpillars.
